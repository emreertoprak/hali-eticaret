## Brand & Style

The design system marries traditional Anatolian textile craftsmanship with high-converting, modern e-commerce engineering. The visual character communicates warmth, generational heritage, authenticity, and premium domestic comfort. 

The aesthetic sits at the intersection of **Tactile Warmth** and **Contemporary Retail Minimalism**:
- **Clean Structure with Rich Details:** Crisp modular layouts allow intricate pile textures, kilim weaves, and natural wool fibers to remain the primary focus.
- **Warm Architectural Foundation:** Avoiding sterile clinical whites, the design deploys subtle stone, sand, and cream backgrounds reminiscent of historic stone bazaars and washed linen.
- **Cultural Precision:** Turkish Red acts as a sovereign punctuation mark, while deep charcoal provides solid retail structure, softened by welcoming golden accents.

## Colors

The palette balances cultural heritage, high-clarity promotional visibility, and luxurious editorial restraint.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#C8102E`):** Turkish Red. Reserved strictly for cultural identity points, top-level brand flags, critical badges (e.g., "El Dokuması", "Tükeniyor"), and decisive urgency indicators.
- **Secondary (`#FFC94D`):** Warm Golden Amber. Powers the commercial engine: primary conversion buttons, installment messaging highlights, and limited-time offer badges. Always paired with `#222222` text for WCAG AAA contrast.
- **Tertiary / Base Dark (`#1F1F1F`):** Deep Charcoal. Establishes grounding contrast across primary typography, navigation states, and the authoritative multi-column footer.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F3F1EC`):** Sandstone / Cream. Anchors the header, category pills, and background fills, eliminating harsh digital glare.

### Supporting & Functional Tones
- **Subtle Surface & Utility Tones:** Announcement band (`#E9E9E9`), category ribbon strip (`#E4E4E4`), structural card borders (`#E8E6DF`), and clean card backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Typography Neutrals:** Primary body & headers (`#222222`), muted specifications & breadcrumbs (`#666666`).
- **Omnichannel & Engagement Tokens:**
  - WhatsApp Floating Contact: `#25D366` with pure white iconography.
  - Story-Ring Highlight: `linear-gradient(45deg, #f09433 0%, #e6683c 25%, #dc2743 50%, #cc2366 75%, #bc1888 100%)`.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Nunito Sans** with **Noto Serif** to distinguish fast, modern transactional flows from evocative textile provenance.

- **Noto Serif (Editorial & Luxury):** Applied exclusively to top-level editorial collection banners, heritage masterheads, artisan provenance blocks, and product titles in flagship ranges (e.g., Oushak, Hereke, Vintage Kilim).
- **Nunito Sans (Commerce & Interface):** Used for navigation bars, filtering systems, prices, spec sheets, reviews, button CTAs, and promotional ribbons. Its subtle curvature softens data-heavy e-commerce pages.
- **Eyebrow Style:** Category tags, tracking details, and micro-ribbons use `label-eyebrow` in uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.12em`) to guarantee legibility over colored background strips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a fluid 12-column desktop grid with a maximum content container of `1440px`.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, and `3rem` (`48px`) outer page margin. Generous vertical breathing room between merchandising modules (`4rem` to `6rem`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, `1rem` gutters, and `1.5rem` margins. Product grids collapse from 4 columns to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters, and `1rem` (`16px`) outer canvas margins. Category stories switch to horizontal snap-scroll rails.

### Stacked Promotional Header Architecture
Top-of-page elements follow a strict vertical hierarchy:
1. **Red Accent Line:** Height `4px`, color `#C8102E`.
2. **Announcement Strip:** Height `32px`, `#E9E9E9`, `body-sm` centered text.
3. **Primary Header:** Height `80px`, `#F3F1EC`, housing the logo, search bar, and utility icons.
4. **Installment Bar:** Height `36px`, `#FFC94D`, bold dark `#222222` center-locked notification.
5. **Collection Banner:** Height `44px`, `#E4E4E4`, uppercase tracked link ("TÜM HALILAR İÇİN TIKLAYINIZ").

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy synthetic drop shadows in favor of crisp outlines and natural, sunlight-inspired ambient diffusion. Rugs and carpets possess physical pile depth; UI surfaces mimic that tactile reality.

- **Ground Level (Elevation 0):** Flat background fills (`#F3F1EC` or `#FFFFFF`) bounded by a hairline border: `1px solid #E8E6DF`.
- **Card Rest State (Elevation 1):** `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.05)`, framed with `1px solid #E8E6DF`.
- **Card Hover State (Elevation 2):** Subtle upward lift (`translateY(-2px)`) with expanded shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.08)`. Border remains `#E8E6DF`.
- **Modals, Floating Trays & Drawers (Elevation 3):** `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -10px rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.16)`.
- **Floating WhatsApp Anchor:** High-contrast elevation to float above merchandising assets: `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -2px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **pill-shaped (Level 3)** paradigm for interactive targets, balanced by gently rounded rectangular containers for product imagery.

- **Primary CTAs & Category Pills:** Complete pill radius (`9999px` / `rounded-xl`).
- **Product & Content Cards:** Softened structural corners (`12px` / `0.75rem`) to maintain edge alignment for rectangular carpet photography without clipping corner weaves or fringe details.
- **Story Ring Avatars:** Perfect circular aspect ratio (`50%`) surrounded by a `2px` white gap and a `2.5px` Instagram-style gradient border.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#FFC94D`, text `#222222`, weight `700`. Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), vertical padding `0.875rem`, horizontal padding `2rem`. Hover state: slight brightness reduction (`filter: brightness(0.96)`) and subtle lift.
- **Secondary / Cart Trigger:** Border `1.5px solid #222222`, transparent background, text `#222222`, pill-shaped. Hover: `#1F1F1F` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Heritage / Luxury CTA:** Crimson `#C8102E` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, designed for limited artisanal collections.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** White (`#FFFFFF`) interior background enclosed by a `1px solid #E8E6DF` border.
- **Visual Ratio:** 3:4 aspect ratio for carpet assets with subtle zoom-on-hover (`scale(1.03)`).
- **Badging:** Corner badges anchored `8px` from card edges. Craft badges (e.g., "Yün & İpek") use `#F3F1EC` with `#222222` text; promotional badges use `#C8102E` with white text.
- **Size Selector:** Mini rounded pill swatches (`80x150`, `120x180`, `160x230`) placed directly beneath the price label.

### Category Story Rings
- Circular avatar nodes (`72px` desktop, `60px` mobile).
- Surrounded by the signature gradient ring: `linear-gradient(45deg, #f09433, #e6683c, #dc2743, #cc2366, #bc1888)`.
- Separated from the thumbnail by a `2px` solid `#F3F1EC` mask ring.
- Label: `body-sm` centered directly below, restricted to 1 line with truncation.

### Input Fields & Search
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E6DF`, border-radius `9999px` for search bars; `8px` for checkout input blocks.
- Active focus state: `border-color: #222222`, outline `2px solid rgba(255, 201, 77, 0.4)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Checkboxes feature rounded corners (`4px`), `#222222` checked fill with a crisp white checkmark.
- Radio buttons feature a `#222222` outer ring with an inner `#FFC94D` indicator dot.

### WhatsApp Floating Action Component
- Fixed at `bottom: 24px`, `right: 24px`, z-index `90`.
- Outer circle: `56px` diameter, background `#25D366`, pure white SVG icon (`28px`).
- Includes an animated subtle ping indicator during active business hours.

### Footer
- Background `#1F1F1F`, typography `#E9E9E9`, muted links `#A0A0A0`.
- 4-column information grid topped by an artisanal carpet motif separator line.
- Base row features bank installment accreditation badges and secured SSL icons in uniform monochrome containers.
